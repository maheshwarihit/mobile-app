Delete your VAgeWell Care account

In the app

  1. Open VAgeWell Care and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account at the bottom, then Delete permanently.

Without the app

Call or WhatsApp us on +91 93427 03376 from the mobile number you registered with, and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm once it is done.

What is deleted

Your account and login, your profile, your family members, your bookings, and the photos, payment screenshots and reports stored with them. Meal and report results saved on your phone are removed from that phone when you delete the account in the app.
